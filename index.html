Sara Ghazanfari

Ph.D. Candidate @ NYU · Incoming Senior Applied ML Researcher @ Apple

I'm a Ph.D. candidate in Electrical and Computer Engineering at New York University, advised by Siddharth Garg and Farshad Khorrami in the EnSuRe Research Group. My research is on how multimodal large language models perceive, align, and reason about visual content, most recently temporal and cross-video reasoning in video MLLMs.

I've interned at Adobe (2025), building evaluation frameworks for visually-guided image editing, and at Apple (2026), working on video temporal grounding. Starting November 16, 2026, I'm joining Apple as a Senior Applied ML Researcher on the Video Apps team.

Publications

SYNCR: Diagnosing and Learning Cross-Video Reasoning from Simulation

S. Ghazanfari, S. Garg, P. Krishnamurthy, F. Khorrami

Preprint, 2026

A simulator-grounded framework for diagnosing and learning cross-video reasoning. Fine-tuning on SYNCR nearly doubles accuracy and transfers to temporal ordering on real footage.

PDF / arXiv

SYNCR: A Cross-Video Reasoning Benchmark with Synthetic Grounding

S. Ghazanfari, S. Garg, P. Krishnamurthy, F. Khorrami

NeurIPS 2026 Workshop · Oral

A controlled synthetic benchmark for cross-video reasoning with programmatically verified grounding: 8,163 multi-video QA pairs built with Habitat, Kubric, and CLEVRER.

PDF / arXiv / Code

Chain-of-Frames: Advancing Video Understanding in Multimodal LLMs via Frame-Aware Reasoning

S. Ghazanfari, F. Croce, N. Flammarion, P. Krishnamurthy, F. Khorrami, S. Garg

CVPR 2026

Video LLMs whose reasoning steps are grounded in, and explicitly refer to, the relevant frames, outperforming baselines without complex inference pipelines.

PDF / arXiv / Code

SpotEdit: Evaluating Visually-Guided Image Editing Methods

S. Ghazanfari, W. Lin, H. Tian, E. Yumer

NeurIPS 2025 Workshop

A benchmark for visually-guided image editing that exposes how leading models, including GPT-4o, hallucinate visual cues.

PDF / arXiv / Code

Towards Unified Benchmark and Models for Multi-Modal Perceptual Metrics

S. Ghazanfari, S. Garg, N. Flammarion, P. Krishnamurthy, F. Khorrami, F. Croce

CVPR 2025 Workshop

UniSim-Bench tracks the progress of perceptual similarity metrics across uni- and multimodal tasks. We propose UniSim, a set of multi-task models that lay the groundwork for general-purpose perceptual metrics.

PDF / arXiv / Code

EMMA: Efficient Visual Alignment in Multi-Modal LLMs

S. Ghazanfari, A. Araujo, P. Krishnamurthy, S. Garg, F. Khorrami

TMLR 2025

A lightweight module that fuses visual and textual encodings with minimal added parameters, producing instruction-aware visual representations that boost performance and reduce hallucinations.

PDF / arXiv / Code

LipSim: A Provably Robust Perceptual Similarity Metric

S. Ghazanfari, A. Araujo, P. Krishnamurthy, F. Khorrami, S. Garg

ICLR 2024

We show that state-of-the-art perceptual similarity metrics are vulnerable to adversarial attacks and propose LipSim, trained with 1-Lipschitz networks, a robust metric with provable guarantees.

PDF / arXiv / Code

R-LPIPS: An Adversarially Robust Perceptual Similarity Metric

S. Ghazanfari, S. Garg, P. Krishnamurthy, F. Khorrami, A. Araujo

ICML 2023 Workshop

We show LPIPS is sensitive to adversarial perturbation and propose R-LPIPS, which uses adversarially trained deep features to be robust where the standard metric is not.

PDF / arXiv / Code
